Stations of the Journey

Condemned Before Pilate
Sorrow

Pilate finds no fault, yet surrenders him to the crowd's cry. Jesus stands silent — dignity unbroken amid the injustice.

The Cross Is Laid Upon Him
Agony

The weight of the beam — rough, splintered wood — presses into wounds already open. Every step a deliberate choice of love.

He Falls the First Time
Agony

The stone path rushes up. Knees strike the ground. The crowd gasps. He rises — not from strength alone, but from will.

He Meets His Mother
Sorrow

Eyes meet — a mother's and a son's. No words are enough. Mary's heart, as foretold, is pierced by a sword of grief.

Simon of Cyrene Helps
Compassion

A stranger pressed into service becomes an instrument of mercy. One act of bearing another's burden — etched in eternity.

Veronica Wipes His Face
Compassion

A woman breaks through the crowd. She offers her veil. In the tender gesture of relief, love answers suffering.

He Falls Again — Twice More
Agony

The body reaches its limit. But the spirit refuses surrender. Each rising is a testament — love is stronger than suffering.

Arrival at Golgotha
Love

The hill of the skull. The place of execution. What Roman law meant as shame, love transforms into the altar of sacrifice.

The Cross Is Raised
Hope

Lifted between earth and heaven — the cross becomes a bridge. From this moment, suffering is never without meaning again.
