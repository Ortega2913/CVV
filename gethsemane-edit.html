"Why did Jesus choose
the dark garden…
instead of the safe Temple?"

He walked into the darkest night of His life…

Garden of Gethsemane

…and fell with His face to the ground, bearing what no one else could carry.

"Gethsemane" — the oil press.
Olives must be crushed
to release something precious.

"He went to be crushed
so you don't have to
face life alone."

"Not my will, but Yours."
